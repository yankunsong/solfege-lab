What is jianpu?

Jianpu is numbered musical notation, widely used in China and East Asia. The digits 1–7 stand for the seven scale degrees, sung as do re mi fa sol la si. In C major, 1 = C, 2 = D, 3 = E, and so on. Reading the numbers straight as syllables is the first step to sight-singing from jianpu.
